Comparing teams and players

  • Add: tap + next to a Search result, or + on a row in the table below. A greyed + means it's already in the comparison.
  • Remove one: tap × on its row in a Comparing table.
  • Remove all teams or all players: the Clear next to "Comparing N teams" or "Comparing N players".
  • Remove everything: the Clear next to Search. It also goes back to your saved division and team.
  • The comparison stays when you change Season or Week. Anyone not in that week shows as a dashed "(not in this week)" tag; its × removes it.

Sorting

  • Tap a column heading to sort by it, highest first. Tap it again to flip the order. ▼ or ▲ marks the column the table is sorted by; the faint ⇅ marks the others you can sort by.
  • Team sorts A-Z; Player sorts by player ID (division, then number).
  • Team totals start in standings order: best Record first, ties broken by Points.
  • Each table sorts on its own. Changing Division, or the Clear next to Search, puts the table back in its starting order.
