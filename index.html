What this tool does

The RDL Newsletter Reader turns each week's TONs newsletter into tables you can sort, filter and compare. Look up any team or player's stats, see who plays whom and where, read the predictions and hot darts, check the trophy darts and leader boards, follow how a team did in every match, and trace a player's whole career back to 1996. Pick a Season and a View, then narrow it with the other dropdowns. Your Season, week, Division and Team are remembered for next time.

Stats

  • View Stats is the main one: every team's season-to-date record, points and win percentages by game type, and, if you want them, each player's too. It works for every season back to Sp96 (an older season shows its final standings).
  • Pick a Division, then a Team, then a Player. Player starts on None (just the teams); All players adds everyone.
  • Show (the checkboxes) picks the column groups: Standings, Singles, Doubles, Overall, All-Star and Tiebreakers.
  • Search finds a team or player by any part of the name or ID.

Match Results

  • How a team did in each match this season, game type by game type (301, SCkt, DCkt, 501), with the ASPs and the score. Each pair of numbers is the picked team's, then its opponent's (with All teams, the home team's, then the away team's). It covers every week reported so far, and a bye week gets its own row.
  • Pick a Division and a Team, or All teams for every match in the division, one table per week with that week's headlines under it (Home is listed first, then Away).
  • Opponent shows just the matches against one team (both times you played them); Match Week shows one week, even if you don't know who it was against. Picking one puts the other back on All.

Schedule

  • Who plays whom each week, home and away, where, and any bye. Match Week (1-14) picks the week.
  • Locations link to a map when the bar is on the approved list; anything else is plain text.

Predictions

  • Each division's match write-ups and picks from the newsletter, for the TONs Week picked.

Hot Darts

  • The week's hot darts for each division: 9-mark rounds, bulls, ASPs and fast games, as the newsletter lists them.

Trophy Darts

  • The Divisional Trophy Darts: High In, High Out and Fast games.
  • Category picks one, or All for a table of each.

Perfect Throws

  • The week's perfect throws, a table for each category (9-Marks, Ton-80s, 6-Bulls) in the order the newsletter prints them: the player, the team and the date or dates, as the newsletter lists them. A number in parentheses is how many times.
  • Division picks one division or All. This view starts with Fa26.

Leader Boards

  • The leaders in Singles win percentage, Singles + Doubles win percentage and All-Star Point Average (ASP). Category picks one, or All for all three.
  • A blank rank is tied with the one above.

Career

  • Every season a player played, back to Sp96, from each season's final standings (the current season so far), with a Career total at the bottom.
  • Search a player under Player; each one you pick adds a table, so you can compare careers. × takes one away, Clear takes them all.
  • The graph above the tables shows one stat, picked under Graph, with a line for each player. Over picks a whole career (a point per season) or one season (a point per week, season to date). The dashed lines are each player's average.
  • Point at the graph (on a phone, tap it or slide along it) to see the numbers; tap outside it to close them. Collapse shrinks the graph, like a table.
  • The newsletters spelled some names differently over the years (Jerry / Gerald). Names that look like the same person count as one career; Jr. and Sr. are kept apart.
  • Each row in the table shows the Season, Division and Team for reference.
  • Send requests for changes (spelling, etc) to rdlstatistics@gmail.com

Comparing teams and players

  • Add: tap + next to a Search result, or + on a row in any table. Rows already in the comparison are highlighted in green, and their + turns into a −: tap − to take it back out.
  • Remove one: tap × on its row in the comparison.
  • Remove all teams or all players: the Clear next to the compared teams' or players' names.
  • Remove everything: Reset (next to Search) clears ALL current comparisons, every team and player at once, and goes back to your saved division and team.
  • The comparison stays when you change Season or Week. Anyone not in that week shows as a dashed "(not in this week)" tag; its × removes it.
  • Collapse (top-left of every table) shrinks that table to its top row, e.g. the table you pick from so the comparison is easier to see; Expand brings it back. Picking something new expands them all.

Sorting

  • Tap a column heading to sort by it, highest first. Tap it again to flip the order. ▼ or ▲ marks the column the table is sorted by; the faint ⇅ marks the others you can sort by.
  • Team sorts A-Z; Player sorts by player ID (division, then number).
  • Team totals start in standings order: best Record first, ties broken by Points (while Standings is checked).
  • Each table sorts on its own. Changing Division, or Reset, puts the table back in its starting order.
